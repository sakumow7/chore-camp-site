# Chore Camp Privacy Policy

_Effective date: 10/05/2026

Chore Camp is a chore and allowance tracker for families. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. The short version: **everything stays on your device. We don't collect, receive, sell or share any of your information.**

## What the app stores, and where

To work, Chore Camp saves the following **only on the device where it's installed**:

- Names you enter for family members, and the emoji avatar each person picks
- Each person's PIN, stored as a salted one-way hash (the PIN itself is never saved)
- Chores (title, description, reward amount, how often they repeat)
- Chore assignments, completion and approval status, notes between parents and kids, and earnings totals
- Simple usage counters (for example, how many times the app has been opened). These never leave the device.

Chore Camp has no servers and no online accounts. The app does not send any of this information to us or to anyone else.

## What we don't do

- We don't collect personal information from anyone, including children.
- We don't show ads.
- We don't use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
- We don't sell or share data.
- The app doesn't ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone or other device permissions.

## Device backup

If you have turned on Android's backup feature, Android may include Chore Camp's data in your device backup to your Google account, and restore it when you set up a new device. This is controlled by your device settings and handled by Google under its own privacy policy. We can't access these backups.

## Children

Chore Camp is designed to be set up and managed by a parent or guardian, and is used by children in their family. Because the app keeps all information on the family's device and doesn't transmit it, we don't collect personal information from children. Parents control which accounts exist on the device and can remove all of the app's data at any time (see below).

## Deleting your data

You can delete all of Chore Camp's data at any time by uninstalling the app, or by going to **Settings > Apps > Chore Camp > Storage > Clear storage** on your device.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date above. If a future version of Chore Camp starts collecting or sharing any information (for example, to sync between devices), we'll update this policy before that version is released.
